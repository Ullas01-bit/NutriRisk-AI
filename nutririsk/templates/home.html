The Clinical Imperative

Why Early Nutritional Risk Assessment Matters

Malnutrition is a complex, multi-factorial condition driven by inadequate intake, monotonous diets, chronic health conditions, and socioeconomic disparities.

Overcoming Single-Metric Blindspots

Traditional clinical screenings rely overwhelmingly on Body Mass Index (BMI). However, individuals with normal or elevated BMI can still suffer from micronutrient deficiencies, sarcopenia, or poor dietary quality that single-indicator measurements miss.

Preventative Clinical Stratification

Stratifying patients into differentiated risk cohorts enables healthcare providers to prioritize targeted nutritional counseling and proactive interventions before acute clinical complications occur.

Scalable Public Health Decision Support

By collecting non-invasive demographic, dietary diversity, and lifestyle parameters, NutriRisk AI can be deployed in community health settings without requiring invasive or expensive laboratory equipment.

Risk Stratification

Understanding Risk Classification Tiers

The system computes posterior probabilities across three clinically distinct risk tiers.

Low Risk Tier 1

Adequate Nutritional Health

Nutritional intake, dietary diversity scores, and anthropometric metrics are balanced. No acute clinical or socioeconomic vulnerabilities are present.

Recommended Action: Routine periodic health monitoring; maintenance of current dietary diversity.
Moderate Risk Tier 2

Emerging Nutritional Vulnerability

Sub-optimal indicators present, such as monotonous food groups, low meal frequency, recent acute illness, or socioeconomic barriers.

Recommended Action: Nutritional counseling, dietary diversification plan, follow-up screening in 3 months.
High Risk Tier 3

Acute Malnutrition Susceptibility

High probability of undernutrition or severe metabolic strain driven by combined anthropometric deficits, extreme dietary insufficiency, illness, and poverty.

Recommended Action: Prompt clinical evaluation by a medical practitioner or specialized dietitian.
