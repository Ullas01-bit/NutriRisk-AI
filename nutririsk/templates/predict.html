Pediatric Scope Limitation (Under 18)

Subject Under 18 Detected

NutriRisk AI is specifically calibrated, validated, and clinically designed for adult populations aged 18 and older.

Clinical Protocol Warning: Pediatric malnutrition assessment cannot be accurately evaluated using adult Body Mass Index (BMI) formulas. Pediatric screening requires WHO Child Growth Standards (weight-for-age, height-for-age z-scores, and Mid-Upper Arm Circumference [MUAC]).

To maintain clinical safety and ethical standards, this screening cannot be processed for an underage subject.
