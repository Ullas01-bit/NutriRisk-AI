Clinical Longitudinal Decision-Support Guidance
NutriRisk AI produces mathematical probability distributions and risk stratification categories based on self-reported clinical and lifestyle parameters collected at a specific point in time. Variations or shifts in predicted risk category across sequential assessment dates do NOT constitute proof of clinical recovery, health improvement, or disease progression. Model outputs represent screening aids to prioritize nutritional triage and must be interpreted in conjunction with formal clinical history, biochemical diagnostic markers, anthropometric skinfold measurements, and licensed medical consultation.
